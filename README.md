# Wizard Traveler

**A Canceled Project**: *Wizard Traveler* is a game created for the **Summer Jam 2024** by Mlabs. Embark on an epic adventure in *Wizard Traveler*, where you step into the shoes of a powerful wizard on a heroic quest to save the realm from the undead menace! In this stationary, level-based game, you'll face fixed enemies and allies at the start of each stage, putting your strategic skills to the test.

Utilizing an innovative slingshot mechanic, you’ll launch support potions that wield transformative effects like gigantification, healing, double damage, multiplication, and division. Your aim matters—carefully target your allies to empower them or strike enemies to weaken their forces. With each level completed by vanquishing all foes, your tactical decision-making is crucial; if all your units fall, it's game over!

*Wizard Traveler* offers an exhilarating blend of fast-paced action and strategic gameplay. Each level immerses you in colorful visuals and thrilling challenges, rewarding you with a sense of accomplishment as you cleanse the world of darkness. With intuitive controls and dynamic mechanics, *Wizard Traveler* appeals to both casual gamers and strategic thinkers alike.

Join the quest, harness the power of potions, and become the hero the world needs in *Wizard Traveler*! Are you ready to take on the challenge and save the realm?

## Reflection

We should have used GitHub for collaboration instead of Unity Cloud and its version control, which had a limit of 3 people per team for free accounts, unlike GitHub. Also, we should have scheduled more meetings with the assigned mentor about ideas and mock-up images instead of first completing our prototype and then asking for feedback. Also, we should have been more clear about the assigned workload along with the deadlines.
